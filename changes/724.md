### Added

- Google Ads settings are now recognised in `.env`: `GOOGLE_ADS_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_ADS_CLIENT_SECRET`, `GOOGLE_ADS_REDIRECT_URI`, `GOOGLE_ADS_TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, and an optional `GOOGLE_ADS_LOGIN_CUSTOMER_ID`. All are optional and separate from Drive: leaving them blank changes nothing, and a value that is present but unusable (a wrong callback path, a short key, or a key reused from Drive) stops startup with a message naming the Ads variable. Nothing connects to Google Ads yet.
- The local database gains the tables that will hold Google Ads accounts, campaigns, and daily figures, with approval and client choices kept apart from what Google reports. Existing databases upgrade automatically and keep their data.
- The activity log can now record Google Ads connect, disconnect, and sync events.

### Breaking changes

None.
