## Added

- Signal plans carry a **lifecycle** (`Active` / `Retired`) and **delivery provenance** (`In
  Signal` / `Outside of Signal`) beside planning status — never as fourth or fifth status values.
- **Retire plan** replaces the conflated Delete control: retired plans leave ordinary planner,
  calendar, and queue-health views while publication history stays; withdraw remains only on the
  provider reconcile panel.
- Planner filters for active, retired, or all plans (lifecycle dimension), with counts labelled
  accordingly.

## Changed

- Hard-delete refuses posts with publication history and never withdraws a provider submission as a
  side effect of removing a plan.

## Breaking changes

None.
