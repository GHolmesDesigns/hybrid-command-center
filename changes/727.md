### Added

- Google Ads performance snapshots. A new **refresh** (`POST /api/ads/performance/refresh`) reads every approved Ads account the connected Google account still reaches and stores its campaigns and the latest 90 days of the account's own figures: impressions, clicks, cost and conversions, exactly as Google reports them. Campaigns with no activity are still listed, and a day Google did not report is left out rather than shown as zero.
- A refresh replaces the stored snapshot all at once or not at all. If any account, stream or row fails, or a limit is hit, the previous figures stay exactly as they were, the failure is recorded in the activity log, and the last-sync state says the figures are from an earlier refresh.
- `GET /api/ads/performance` returns the stored snapshot with each account's currency and time zone beside its figures. It reads only local data and never contacts Google.

### Breaking changes

None.
