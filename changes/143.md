### Changed

- Project tiles now show which status they are in. Planning, active, on hold, complete, and
  archived each have their own chip colour, icon, and tile tint, so a mixed Projects list
  separates at a glance instead of having to be read one word at a time. Every status keeps its
  word and its own icon shape, so the five stay apart in greyscale or with colours turned off,
  and each one clears AA contrast on the chip and on the tile. Archived projects stay recessive
  — the one neutral tint, and the only dashed edge — without becoming any harder to read.

### Breaking changes

None.
