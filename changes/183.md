## Fixed

- Settings no longer leaves a blank strip above a card. The page's two columns used to share
  rows, so the taller of any pair decided where the next card on the *other* side began:
  connecting Google Drive, a validation message appearing, or a long list of categories pushed
  the card beside it down and left a gap above it. Each column is now its own stack, and a card
  moves only when something above it in its own column changes size.

## Changed

- Settings reads in one order at every width: Google Drive, Project categories, Task tags,
  Sidebar branding, Local timezone, Calendar. On a wide screen that is the left column and then
  the right; on a narrow screen the two columns become one and the cards follow in the same
  order, which is also the order the keyboard and a screen reader move through them. Branding
  now sits at the top of the right-hand column rather than second on the page.

## Breaking changes

None.
