## Added

- **Signal campaigns**: a shared list of names that group Signal posts, the way project categories
  group projects. A post can carry as many as it needs — the campaign *and* the week inside it —
  where before it had one free-text line that could only hold whichever of the two you typed.
- Campaigns are typed straight into a post, under **Campaigns**, as chips. A name already in the
  workspace is suggested as you type and picked when you choose it; a new one is created when the
  post is saved. Capitalisation and stray spaces do not make a second campaign: `Clarity Campaign`
  on one post and `clarity campaign` on another are one campaign, listed under whichever spelling
  was used first.
- **Settings › Signal campaigns** lists every campaign beside how many posts carry it, and is where
  one is added, renamed, or removed. **Renaming reaches every post at once**, because the name lives
  on the campaign and not on the posts. **Deleting only removes the label** — every post that carried
  it stays where it was, on its day, with its planning status, and reads under **No campaign**
  afterwards. When posts are attached you are told how many before anything happens.
- **Campaign figures**, below the planner: what the platforms counted, added up per campaign — views,
  likes, comments, and shares — with a compact daily trend under them showing what each day *added*
  rather than a running total, and the same days as a table beside it.
- Four filters, each of which stays in the address so a comparison can be bookmarked or shared and a
  reload lands on the same answer: **campaigns**, **channels**, **accounts**, and a **From**/**To**
  date range. Several campaigns are read as *or* — a post in any of them is counted. **Clear filters**
  resets all four and leaves the month the planner is showing exactly where it was.
- **No campaign** is a group you can see and a filter you can select, so a post you never classified
  is never hidden.

## Changed

- The Signal calendar and planner now show **every** campaign a post belongs to rather than one line
  of text, and duplicating a post joins it to the same campaigns rather than copying their names.
- Existing campaign text is converted automatically the first time the application starts after this
  release: each distinct name becomes a campaign, and every post that carried it is attached to it.
  Two spellings of one name resolve to a single campaign under the spelling the earliest post used.
  Nothing is deleted, and the conversion is safe to have run more than once.
- The archive import (`npm run signal:import`) now writes campaigns as part of the same import, and
  remains safe to run twice: it resolves a name against the list you already have rather than adding
  a second spelling, and it never revisits a post it did not just create.
- The application's campaign figures come from the numbers a post's own **Refresh figures** already
  stored. Opening the campaign panel, or moving a filter, contacts no platform and cannot use up a
  synchronisation.

## Notes

- **A campaign total says what it covers.** Every campaign reports how many of its deliveries are
  measured beside its figures — *3 of 5 deliveries measured* — and a campaign with nothing measured
  says so in words and shows no numbers at all. A row of zeros would claim nobody watched; what is
  true is that nobody has looked yet.
- **Nothing is calculated beyond adding up.** A campaign figure is the platforms' own per-delivery
  numbers summed, and a trend point is the sum of the per-day gains already derived from the
  platforms' own daily snapshots. There is no rate, no average, and no per-post figure anywhere.
- **A date range asks which posts, not which days.** A post scheduled inside the range brings its
  whole measured history, so a total is never half of one post's numbers. A post still in the
  unscheduled queue has no date and is in no range.
- Campaigns label Signal posts only. Task tags and project categories are untouched, and the three
  lists stay separate — a workspace organises its content by different words than its work.

## Breaking changes

- **A Signal post's `campaign` field is replaced by `campaigns` in the API.** Anything reading
  `GET /api/signal/posts`, `/api/signal/queue`, `/api/signal/posts/:id`, or `/api/calendar` now gets
  a `campaigns` array of `{ id, name }` instead of a single `campaign` string, and the write routes
  take `campaigns` as an array of names instead of `campaign` as one string. This application's own
  interface is updated throughout; a private script or bookmarklet reading the old field is the only
  thing that would need changing. No stored data is lost — the previous values are converted into the
  new list on first start, and the column that held them is left in place untouched.
