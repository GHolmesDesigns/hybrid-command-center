### Added

- **Signal now has a second, cheaper way to ask about figures: what the provider reports for one
  platform over one of its own windows.** A new **What the provider reports over a window** panel sits
  beside the provider inventory on the planner. It opens on stored rows and contacts nothing; one press
  of **Refresh window** reads every page and then replaces that platform and window outright, or
  replaces nothing at all and says why.
- Each account in the panel reports how many of the deliveries this workspace knows about the window
  actually named, beside the provider's own four counts added over them. An account the window named
  nothing for carries no total rather than a row of zeros.
- Rows the provider names that match no delivery recorded here are counted and shown as unattributed,
  and never folded into an account's figures.
- Reading a window records one entry in the integration activity log — **Signal analytics window
  refresh** — which succeeded or failed as a whole and never partly.

### Changed

- Nothing about the per-post **Figures** panel. Per-delivery figures, their daily history, and their
  provenance are stored, refreshed, and displayed exactly as before; a window is a second question
  about the same provider, not a replacement for the first.

### Known limitations

- **No window is offered yet, and the panel says so rather than guessing.** The provider accepts a
  window filter, but what that filter selects — which posts are included, or which measurement days
  are counted — has not been observed against a live account, so offering `7d` or `30d` would put a
  label on a total nobody can describe. The panel explains this in place of the control. When a dated
  provider result records what a window means, that window appears with the meaning the result
  recorded, and nothing else in the app has to change.
- A window read is asked for by a person and never by a timer, and it cannot ask the provider to
  refresh its own copy of the figures. Opening the panel or changing its selection spends nothing.

### Breaking changes

None.
