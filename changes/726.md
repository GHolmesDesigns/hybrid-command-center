### Added

- **Settings → Ads accounts** lists the Google Ads accounts the connected grant reaches directly. Listing reads nothing about them. You approve one exact serving account, and only then does the app read that account's details. Manager and cancelled accounts are refused as performance targets, and you can withdraw an approval later.
- An approved account can be mapped to a client, reassigned, or unassigned through a preview you confirm. A change to the account or client after the preview makes the confirmation refuse. Unassigned accounts stay listed under their own heading.
- If Google Ads is disconnected, or a later listing no longer reaches an approved account, its last details stay on screen marked stale and nothing is read for it. Reconnecting and listing again clears the mark.

### Changed

- Archiving a client keeps its Ads account links. Merging a client moves its Ads accounts to the surviving client in the same step.

### Breaking changes

None.
