## Added

- `docs/signal-import-format.md` — a register of what Wave 16 Signal import deliberately leaves
  unbuilt: `[SignalTargets]`, export, scheduling intelligence, playbook union, and the permanent
  never-publishes boundary. Each entry carries why it is not built and the concrete thing that would
  reopen it. The two deliberate departures from the playbook importer — identity-matched updates,
  and capability verdicts that inform rather than refuse — are recorded with their reasoning so they
  are not reopened as unfinished work. `[SignalVariants]` and variant role media are named as
  shipped rather than unbuilt, because the importer already lands them.
