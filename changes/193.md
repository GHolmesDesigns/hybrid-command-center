## Added

- A post you have already submitted can now be brought back into line with the provider, instead of
  only being cancelled. Editing the post in Signal raises **Provider update required** on its
  delivery record, names the fields that moved — caption, scheduled time, media, or accounts — and
  changes nothing at the provider.
- **Compare with provider** reads what the provider is currently holding and shows it beside what
  Signal holds, field by field, with the rows that disagree marked. Reading the comparison writes
  nothing on either side.
- Four actions, each confirmed on its own: **Update provider content** sends Signal's caption, media,
  accounts, and per-platform content while leaving the provider's time alone; **Update provider
  schedule** moves the time while leaving the content alone; **Cancel provider post** withdraws it;
  and **Restore from Signal and resubmit** withdraws it and sends the post again as a new submission.
  An update keeps the same publication, its provider id, and its permalinks.
- An action that cannot be taken is replaced by the reason, not a greyed-out button. A post the
  provider has already published cannot be cancelled or changed; a post being sent right now is left
  alone until the send finishes; rescheduling is refused when the provider is holding content this
  app did not send, so a reschedule can never quietly overwrite an edit made in Post Bridge; and any
  action is refused when the post could not be published as it now stands, with the same reason the
  publishing preview gives.
- If the post or the provider changes while a comparison is open, confirming is refused and the
  comparison is taken again — so an action is never applied to a difference that has already moved.

## Changed

- The delivery record now snapshots the media and the per-platform content that went out, alongside
  the caption and channels it already kept. That is what lets a comparison weigh what the provider
  was actually handed rather than re-deriving it from a post that has since been edited. Posts you
  published before this release have no such record, and the app says so rather than guessing: their
  comparison notes that the media that went out was never recorded, and only **Update provider
  schedule** is held back, since it is the one action that has to know. Updating the content records
  it and clears the note.
- Each external operation on a provider post records its own line in the integration activity log,
  scrubbed of credentials. A withdraw-and-resend records both halves, so a resend that fails leaves
  the withdrawal recorded and the post ready to retry rather than a half-finished state with nothing
  written down.
- An update the provider never answers is now reported as unconfirmed rather than as a failure, and
  is not retried on its own — the same rule submissions already followed, for the same reason: *we do
  not know* and *it did not happen* call for different responses, and only one of them is safe to
  repeat blindly.

## Breaking changes

None.
