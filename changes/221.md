### Added

- **Signal now shows what Post Bridge is holding, including posts this app did not send.** A new
  **What Post Bridge is holding** panel sits below the planner and lists every post the provider has:
  the state it is in, when it goes out, which accounts it names, and whether this app sent it. A post
  created in the Post Bridge interface, by a VA, or by an agent with its own key was previously
  invisible here — and it is exactly the thing that collides with a slot the planner shows as empty.
- **It is refreshed only when you press Refresh inventory.** Opening the planner reads what was
  stored last time and contacts nothing. A refresh reads every page of the provider's list before it
  stores anything, and then replaces the whole inventory in one step: posts the provider no longer
  lists are dropped, the rest are brought up to date, and every row carries the same read time. A
  refresh that cannot be finished — a page that fails, a list that will not page properly, more posts
  than one read may hold — replaces nothing at all, leaves the previous inventory exactly as it was,
  and says why above it. Each attempt records one honest line in the integration activity log.
- **A new queue-health alert, *Provider inventory*, worth watching.** It reports the posts at the
  provider that no submission from here claims, names the first few, and links to the panel.
  Acknowledging it records that you have seen those posts in those states, so it comes back when one
  of them is published or another one appears.
- **Nothing in the panel writes.** There is one button and it refreshes; everything else is a link
  out to the provider where it supplies an address. Adopting, importing, editing, rescheduling, and
  withdrawing one of the provider's own posts are deliberately not offered here, and the panel says
  so rather than leaving it to be discovered.
