# Logo

The full horizontal logo, and the default mark for formal brand recognition.

**You provide:** `variant`, optional `width` and `clearSpace`. `auto` follows the theme, showing the primary logo on light and the reverse logo on dark.

- Use `primary` on paper-white or light-neutral: website header, proposals, invoices, business cards, client PDFs.
- Use `reverse` only on near-black, charcoal or deep dark grounds: presentation covers, video end cards, dark sections, event signage.
- `width` floors at 120px, the digital minimum. In print the minimum is 1.5 in / 38 mm wide.
- `clearSpace` adds padding on all sides equal to the height of the bulb icon. Keep that zone free of type, graphics and competing edges.

**Don't** place it over busy photography; add shadows, glows, outlines or bevels; stretch, compress, rotate or recolor it; or separate the bulb from the wordmark outside the icon system.
